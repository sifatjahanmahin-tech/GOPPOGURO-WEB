What We Do

One Purpose.

At Goppo Guro, every programme is rooted in the same belief: creativity is not a talent — it is a right. We build spaces where children learn, women create, and communities flourish.

01 Education

Creative Learning Programme

The Goppoguro Creative Learning Programme (GCLP) brings weekly paper-based workshops into schools — turning classrooms into spaces of storytelling, creative making, and joyful expression. Children don't just learn about creativity; they live it.

Built on the 4P Framework — Projects, Passion, Peers, Play — GCLP equips educators and children with the tools, materials, and confidence to explore their inner worlds through paper. Each session ends with a creative exhibition where children celebrate what they have made.

02 Empowerment

Women Makers

The Women Makers Programme is the heartbeat behind Little Joys. Women artisans across communities fold tiny paper objects by hand — hearts, stars, birds, butterflies — earning flexible, fair income from their homes while building craftsmanship and quiet confidence.

Every fold is an act of care. Every purchase funds both the maker's livelihood and a child's education. No factories. No machines. Only time, patience, and human hands.

"Small folds. Meaningful work. Quiet empowerment."

28 Artisans in the Network
1,200 Jars Handcrafted

03 Children

Golpoka Club

Golpoka Club is a creative membership programme for curious children aged 5+. Every month, members receive a thoughtfully curated box filled with storybooks, craft kits, activity materials, storytelling games, and imagination challenges.

More than a subscription — Golpoka Club is a growing community where children become makers, storytellers, dreamers, and young creators. Members join competitions, exhibitions, and publications that celebrate their imagination.

"Small stories can grow big imaginations."

Our Philosophy

Why These Programmes Exist

Creativity is a Right

We believe every child deserves access to creative expression, not just those in well-resourced schools. GCLP takes paper and play directly into classrooms that need it most.

Dignity through Making

Handcraft is not a lesser form of work. The Women Makers Programme recognizes patience, skill, and care as the foundations of meaningful livelihood.

Imagination Shapes the Future

Golpoka Club exists because the children who imagine boldly today become the curious, creative, and confident humans the world needs tomorrow.
