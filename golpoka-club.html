A Creative Membership Club

The Golpoka Club

A creative membership programme by Goppo Guro for curious children aged 5+, where imagination grows through stories, playful learning, creative activities, and hands-on exploration.

More than a subscription — Golpoka Club is a creative journey where children become makers, storytellers, dreamers, explorers, and young creators.

Join the Club See What's Inside
What Is It?

A Monthly Creative Adventure

Golpoka Club is designed for children who love stories, making things, and exploring the world through play. Each month follows a different theme — encouraging children to discover new ideas, stories, cultures, emotions, and ways of thinking.

Every member receives a thoughtfully curated Goppo Guro creative package filled with surprise, wonder, and activities designed to spark imagination, curiosity, and joyful learning.

No special skills needed. Only curiosity.

More Than a Box

A Platform to Create & Share

Members can actively participate, create, and be celebrated. Golpoka Club is a growing community, not just a product.

🏆

Competitions

Fun and inspiring challenges in storytelling, drawing, making, and creative thinking — where effort is celebrated as much as outcome.

🖼️

Exhibitions

Selected works are showcased through exhibitions where children celebrate their creativity with peers, parents, and communities.

📖

Publications

Children's artworks, stories, poems, and creations may be featured in Goppo Guro books, magazines, exhibitions, or digital publications.

🎉

Creative Events

Why It Matters

Why Golpoka Club?

In a world filled with screens and passive entertainment, children need spaces where they can imagine, make, explore, and express. Golpoka Club creates that space — at home, every month, in their own way.

We believe every child deserves the opportunity to explore their own creative world — to feel proud of their ideas, to build confidence through making, and to know that their imagination matters.

Our Dream

A Generation of Young Creators

We dream of building a generation of children who are curious, creative, confident, kind, and playful thinkers. Golpoka Club is a small beginning toward a larger culture of imagination — where creativity is not reserved for art class, but woven into everyday life.

Because creativity grows stronger when shared.
