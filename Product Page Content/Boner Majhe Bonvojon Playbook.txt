2. Boner Majhe Bonvojon (Playbook)
A Story You Can Build & Play
Cut. Fold. Imagine.

Boner Majhe Bonvojon transforms storytelling into a hands-on play experience where children cut, fold, arrange, and create their own forest adventures.

The Story Behind It

We wanted children to stop only consuming stories and start creating stories.

What happens when a tiger meets a monkey?

What if the crocodile joins the picnic?

What happens next?

The child decides.

What Children Learn

✨ Imagination
🧠 Story sequencing
🤝 Problem solving
🎭 Roleplay
🎨 Creative composition
