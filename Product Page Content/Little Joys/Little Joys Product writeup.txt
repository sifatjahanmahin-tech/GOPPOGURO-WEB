1. Little Joys
Tiny Folded Happiness
Small gestures. Big emotions.

Little Joys is a collection of tiny handcrafted paper treasures designed to spread kindness, imagination, and emotional connection through simple acts of giving.

A folded star.
A paper heart.
A tiny bird shared with someone you care about.

Sometimes the smallest things carry the biggest feelings.

The Story Behind Little Joys

In a fast and digital world, we often forget the beauty of tiny human gestures.

A handwritten note.
A handmade gift.
A small surprise shared without reason.

Little Joys was created to bring back moments of warmth through tiny handcrafted objects that invite connection, storytelling, and care.

Each fold becomes a small emotional experience.

The Idea

Little Joys encourages:

❤️ Kindness
✨ Imagination
🤲 Sharing
🎨 Handmade creativity
💭 Emotional expression

Children and adults alike learn that joy can come from very simple things.
