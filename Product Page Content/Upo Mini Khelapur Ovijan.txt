Upo Mini Khelapur Ovijan
A Playful Journey of Stories, Thinking & Imagination
Learn through play. Imagine through stories.

Upo Mini Khelapur Ovijan is a story-driven activity book designed to transform learning into a playful adventure.

Through stories, puzzles, imagination games, observation challenges, and creative activities, children join Upo Mini on a joyful journey through the magical world of Khelapur — a place filled with curiosity, surprises, and possibilities.

Every page invites children to think, imagine, solve, observe, and create.

Not through pressure.

But through play.

The Story Behind It

Children naturally learn best when they are curious, engaged, and emotionally connected.

We wanted to create a world where learning feels like an adventure rather than a task.

That is how Upo Mini Khelapur Ovijan began — a playful story experience where activities become part of the journey and imagination becomes part of learning.

Instead of memorization, children explore ideas through interaction, storytelling, and creative discovery.

What Children Explore

✨ Observation & Focus
🧩 Logical Thinking
🎨 Creativity & Imagination
📖 Storytelling
🔍 Problem Solving
🗣 Expression & Communication

Each activity is carefully designed to help children grow while having fun.

Unlike ordinary activity books, Upo Mini Khelapur Ovijan combines:

Story + Play + Learning

Every puzzle solved, every drawing made, and every challenge completed helps move the story forward.
