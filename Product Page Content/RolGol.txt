RolGol
Unroll Adventure
A simple paper roll that opens into a world of stories.

RolGol is a playful storytelling tool where children can imagine, draw, and create their own comic — frame by frame.

As the paper slowly rolls forward, the story grows with it.

A character appears.
A mystery begins.
An adventure unfolds.

Children can draw scenes, invent characters, finish unfinished stories, or create entirely new worlds through imagination and play.

Inspired by comics, storytelling scrolls, and hands-on creativity, RolGol transforms storytelling into something children can physically touch, turn, and build.

No screens.
No fixed endings.
No “right” answers.

Just imagination in motion.

The Idea

RolGol was created from a simple belief:

Children should not only consume stories — they should create them.

In a world filled with passive entertainment, RolGol invites children back to storytelling through making, drawing, and playful imagination.

Every roll becomes a new possibility.

Every frame becomes part of a bigger story.
