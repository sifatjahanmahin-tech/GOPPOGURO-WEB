বীরপুরুষ (Birpurush)
A Graphic Tale by Rabindranath Tagore
A timeless Bengali classic, reimagined for young imaginations.

Birpurush by Rabindranath Tagore is one of Bengal’s most beloved poetic tales of imagination, bravery, and a child’s heroic fantasy.

Through Goppoguro’s graphic storytelling approach, this timeless classic is transformed into a visually immersive graphic novel experience, where poetry meets illustration and imagination unfolds frame by frame.

The familiar words of Tagore are reintroduced to children through cinematic visuals, expressive storytelling, and emotionally rich imagery — helping young readers connect with literature in a new and meaningful way.

The Story Behind the Project

Many children grow up hearing the name of Rabindranath Tagore, yet often struggle to emotionally connect with classical literature through conventional formats.

We asked:

What if children could experience Bengali literary classics visually?

What if poetry became an adventure?

What if imagination could be seen?

Birpurush was created to bridge the distance between timeless literature and contemporary young readers through the language children naturally respond to — images, stories, and visual emotion.
