01 — Origin

It Started
With Paper

A single piece of paper. Folded once. Then again. The act of folding is where Goppo Guro began — not in an office, not with a plan, but with a quiet belief that something beautiful was possible.

02 — Studio

A Studio
Was Born

From a home corner, the Goppoguro Studio Network grew. What began as one creative space became a living network — bringing workshops, tools, and the permission to make into communities that needed it.

03 — Stories

Stories
Needed
Telling

Children were creating. Drawing. Imagining. But their stories had nowhere to go. Golpoka Club changed that — giving every curious child a box full of materials and a community to share with.

04 — Craft

Hands
Found
Purpose

Women in the community picked up paper. And found not just a craft — but a livelihood. Every fold became income. Every jar became a child's education day. Little Joys was born.

05 — Now

And We're
Still
Beginning

The paper keeps folding. New projects are forming. The question was never "how much?" — it was always "what's next?" Every project you see here is an answer to that question.

01 Education

Goppo Guro
Studio Network

A distributed network of community-based creative studios — bringing paper craft, origami workshops, and hands-on learning into schools, community spaces, and neighbourhoods across Bangladesh.

Each studio is a hub where children, educators, and community members learn to see paper differently — not as waste, but as possibility.

6 Active Studios
2,000 Participants Reached
What it is

The Goppoguro Studio Network establishes creative workspaces within existing community institutions — schools, cultural centres, and neighbourhood spaces. Each studio is equipped with paper, tools, and facilitated programming to run creative sessions year-round.

Who it's for

Children aged 5–16, educators looking to bring creative learning into their classrooms, youth groups, and community organisations in underserved neighbourhoods.

“A studio is not a room. It is a permission to create.”

How it works
01 Partner A school or community space applies to host a Goppoguro Studio.
02 Set Up We supply materials, training, and facilitation support to get the studio running.
03 Run & Grow Weekly workshops run in the space. The studio becomes a permanent creative resource.

Golpoka
Club

A monthly creative subscription for curious children aged 5+. Every month, members receive a thoughtfully curated box — storybooks, craft kits, activity materials, and imagination challenges — delivered to their door.

More than a subscription box, Golpoka Club is a growing community where children become makers, storytellers, and young published authors.

5+ Ages Served
Monthly Creative Deliveries

“Every child is already a storyteller. We just give them the paper.”

What it is

Golpoka Club is a children's creative membership programme. Each monthly box is curated around a theme — adventure, nature, family, dreams — and includes original storybooks, origami kits, drawing challenges, and storytelling games.

Who it's for

Children aged 5 to 14 and their families. Golpoka Club welcomes all curious minds — whether they love to draw, write, fold, or simply listen to a good story.

“Every child is already a storyteller. We just give them the paper.”

How it works
01 Subscribe Families sign up for monthly or quarterly membership online.
02 Receive A curated creative box arrives each month with a surprise theme.
03 Create & Share Children make and share their creations with the Golpoka Club community.

“Small folds. Meaningful work. Quiet empowerment.”

03 Social Enterprise

Little Joys

Handcrafted origami jars made entirely by women artisans. Each jar is folded by hand — hearts, stars, birds, butterflies — and sold to fund both the maker's livelihood and a child's education.

No factories. No machines. Only time, patience, and human hands — creating something beautiful that does something meaningful.

28 Women Artisans
1,200 Jars Handcrafted
What it is

Little Joys is a handcraft commerce initiative. Women in the Goppo Guro network fold tiny paper objects — hearts, stars, birds — and seal them inside small glass jars. Each jar is a product, a livelihood, and a story about dignity and skill.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants beautiful things that do beautiful things. Little Joys jars make thoughtful gifts, desk companions, and gentle reminders that craft has real value.

“Small folds. Meaningful work. Quiet empowerment.”
SDG 1 SDG 8 SDG 12
How it works
