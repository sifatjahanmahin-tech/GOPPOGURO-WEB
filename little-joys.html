Handcrafted with Purpose

A Little Joy in Every Jar

Each Little Joys origami jar is folded by hand by a woman artisan in Bangladesh. Inside: a paper origami figure and a handwritten message. Outside: the knowledge that a child's education is one purchase closer.

What's Inside

More Than a Gift

A Little Joy is a small glass jar containing hand-folded origami figures and slips of paper with messages to spark imagination, kindness, and connection. No instructions. No outcomes. Just presence.

"Keep it or pass it on" — every jar comes with heartfelt messages encouraging the chain of kindness to continue spreading joy wherever it travels.

"There are no timers, no expectations — only moments of presence, imagination, and shared discovery."

— Goppo Guro
The Story Behind It

Folded by Real Hands

Every single Little Joys jar is crafted by a woman artisan working from her home in Bangladesh. No factory. No machine. Just patience, paper, and skill passed from hand to hand.

When you buy a Little Joy, you are paying a fair wage to the woman who made it — and contributing to the welfare fund that sends a child to school.
