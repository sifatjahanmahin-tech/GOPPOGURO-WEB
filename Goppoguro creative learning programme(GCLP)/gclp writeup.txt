Goppoguro Creative Learning Programme (GCLP)
A Pilot Model for Creativity in Primary Education

Helping children imagine, express, and grow through stories, creativity, and joyful learning.

The Goppoguro Creative Learning Programme (GCLP) is a pilot educational initiative designed to introduce storytelling-based creative learning into primary education in Bangladesh.

Through stories, activities, expression, and reflection, GCLP creates joyful learning experiences where children are encouraged to think independently, communicate confidently, and express their imagination freely.

Why GCLP?

Bangladesh has made significant progress in primary education. Yet many classrooms still depend heavily on memorization, leaving limited room for imagination, creativity, and self-expression.

At ages 8–11 (Class 3–5), children are at one of the most important stages for developing:

Creative thinking
Communication skills
Confidence
Problem-solving ability
Emotional expression
Collaboration and empathy

GCLP aims to support this stage through structured, activity-based creative engagement.

How GCLP Works

Every GCLP session follows a simple creative cycle:

Storytelling → Creative Activity → Expression → Reflection

This process encourages children not only to learn — but also to imagine, participate, and communicate their own ideas.

1. Storytelling Session

A facilitator introduces an engaging story that sparks imagination, curiosity, and emotional connection.

Children listen, imagine possibilities, ask questions, and connect with characters and ideas.

Goal: Build imagination and listening skills.

2. Creative Activity & Expression

Children reinterpret the story through:

Drawing
Craft making
Puzzle solving
Group activities
Speaking and storytelling
Creative imagination exercises

There are no “wrong answers” — only different ways of thinking.

Goal: Encourage confidence, creativity, and independent thinking.

3. Reflection & Appreciation

Children share their ideas, artwork, and interpretations with peers and teachers.

Facilitators appreciate participation and encourage children to express themselves freely in a safe and joyful environment.

Goal: Build confidence, empathy, and communication.

Weekly Creative Workshops

Alongside storytelling sessions, students participate in regular creative workshops featuring:

Imagination challenges
Collaborative activities
Drawing & storytelling exercises
Creative games and puzzles
Open-ended thinking sessions

The workshops are designed to make learning feel playful, meaningful, and exciting.

Pilot Programme Overview
Duration

6 Months

Target Group

Class 3–5 (Ages 8–11)

Scale

20 Primary Schools
Approximately 2,000 Students

The programme will be implemented through periodic creative sessions facilitated in collaboration with teachers and school communities.

Expected Outcomes

Through GCLP, we hope to nurture:

Confident Students

Children become more comfortable sharing ideas and expressing themselves.

Creative Thinking

Students learn to imagine, question, and explore possibilities.

Expressive Learning

Children engage with education through participation, creativity, and communication.

Joyful Education

Learning becomes more meaningful, active, and enjoyable.

Creative Exhibition Day

At the end of the programme, students showcase their creative works through a school-wide exhibition where parents, teachers, and communities celebrate children's imagination and expression.

This creates a culture where creativity is seen, valued, and appreciated.

Our Vision

We believe every child carries stories, imagination, and creative potential.

GCLP is an effort to build learning spaces where children are not only taught what to think — but encouraged to think, imagine, create, and express.

“Creativity is not extra learning — it is meaningful learning.”
