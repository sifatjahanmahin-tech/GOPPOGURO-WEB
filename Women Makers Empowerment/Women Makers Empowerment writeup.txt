Women Makers Programme
Made by Hands. Shared by Hearts.
Small folds. Meaningful work. Quiet empowerment.

The Women Makers Programme is a community-centered initiative behind Little Joys, where handcrafted paper creations are lovingly made by women artisans through flexible, home-friendly creative work.

Each Little Joy is more than a folded paper object — it is a moment of care, patience, creativity, and human connection.

Through this programme, women transform simple materials into meaningful keepsakes that carry joy, kindness, and stories from one hand to another.

Why Women Makers?

Many women possess creativity, patience, and extraordinary making skills but often have limited access to flexible income opportunities that fit within their everyday responsibilities.

The Women Makers Programme creates an accessible and dignified form of creative work where women can participate from their own communities while developing craftsmanship, confidence, and financial independence.

We believe meaningful work does not always require factories, machines, or complicated systems.

Sometimes, change begins with hands, patience, and care.

How It Works

Women participants are introduced to the process of creating Little Joys through guided training and simple handcraft techniques.

Each maker carefully folds paper into tiny objects of wonder — hearts, stars, birds, flowers, butterflies, boats, and small symbolic treasures.

These handmade pieces are then thoughtfully packaged as Little Joys and shared through cafés, gifts, community spaces, creative workshops, and everyday moments of kindness.

Every fold becomes part of a larger story of care.

The Story Behind Little Joys

A Little Joy may seem small — a folded heart, a tiny bird, a star tucked into someone’s hand.

But sometimes the smallest gestures carry the deepest meaning.

A paper heart slipped across a café table.

A tiny star gifted to someone having a difficult day.

A folded bird shared simply to say:

“I thought of you.”

Little Joys reminds us that kindness does not need to be expensive, grand, or complicated.

Sometimes joy arrives folded.

100% Handmade

Every Little Joy is carefully folded by hand.

No machines. No factories.

Only time, patience, creativity, and human care.

Because every fold carries a story.

Our Vision

We imagine a future where creativity becomes opportunity, handmade work becomes dignity, and small acts of kindness create meaningful social connection.

Through Little Joys and the Women Makers Programme, we hope to build a system where care is created by hands that matter.

“Every fold carries a story.”
