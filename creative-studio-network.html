Every Week at the Studio

The Weekly Session Schedule

Each week follows a four-session creative rhythm — designed to build imagination, confidence, and expression in children aged 5–12.

Day 1

Storytelling

A facilitator narrates an interactive story that sparks imagination. Children listen, question, and connect with characters and ideas.

Day 2

Creative Activity

Children reinterpret the story through drawing, craft, origami, and hands-on making. No wrong answers — only different ways of thinking.

Day 3

Expression

Students share their artwork, stories, and ideas with peers. They speak, present, and own their creative voice in a safe and joyful space.

Day 4

Reflection & Appreciation

Facilitators appreciate every contribution. Children see that their perspective matters — creativity grows through honesty and openness.

What We Explore

Monthly Creative Theme

Each month the studio centres around a new creative theme — a shared world that all sessions, activities, and exhibitions revolve around. Themes are chosen to spark curiosity and connect to children's everyday lives.

  • Month 1 The World of Animals Stories, origami creatures, and imaginary habitats
  • Month 2 My Dream City Map-making, building, and community imagination
  • Month 3 Colours & Feelings Expressing emotions through colour, shape, and story
  • Month 4 Into the Forest Nature, wonder, and folded paper ecosystems

Every month ends with a mini Creative Exhibition Day — where children showcase their work to parents and the community.

School Collaboration

Bringing Studios into Schools

We partner directly with schools to bring the Goppoguro Creative Studio experience into the classroom. Our in-school pilots integrate the 4P Framework (Projects, Passion, Peers, Play) alongside existing curricula — without replacing it.

Partner schools receive full facilitation support, curated materials, and access to our exhibition calendar. Children gain structured creative time; schools gain measurable engagement outcomes.
