Little Joys is a collection of tiny handcrafted paper treasures designed to spread kindness, imagination, and emotional connection through simple acts of giving.

A folded star. A paper heart. A tiny bird shared with someone you care about. Sometimes the smallest things carry the biggest feelings.

Every Little Joy is handcrafted by women who turn simple paper into moments of care. This work offers flexible income, creative focus, and dignity — without factories or machines. When you pick a piece, you are participating in a system where care is created by hands that matter.
