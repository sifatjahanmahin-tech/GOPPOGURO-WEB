Golpoka Club

A Creative Membership Club for Curious Young Minds

Stories. Play. Creativity. Imagination.

Golpoka Club is a creative membership programme by Goppoguro designed for children aged 5+, where imagination grows through stories, playful learning, creative activities, and hands-on exploration.

More than a subscription — 

Golpoka Club is a creative journey where children become makers, storytellers, dreamers, explorers, and young creators.
Every month, members receive thoughtfully curated Goppoguro materials designed to spark imagination, curiosity, and joyful learning.

What Members Receive

Every Golpoka Club member receives a monthly creative package filled with surprise, wonder, and activities.

Monthly Creative Box Includes:

Goppoguro Storybooks
Creative Activity Books
DIY Craft Kits
Storytelling Games
Imagination Challenges
Hands-on Learning Activities
Seasonal Surprise Materials
Creative Missions for Home

Each month follows a different theme to encourage children to explore new ideas, stories, cultures, emotions, and ways of thinking.
More Than a Box

Golpoka Club is not only about receiving creative materials.
It is a growing platform where children can actively participate, create, and share.

Members can join:

Creative Competitions

Fun and inspiring challenges in storytelling, drawing, making, imagination, and creative thinking.

Exhibitions

Selected works are showcased through exhibitions where children celebrate their creativity with peers, parents, and communities.

Publication Opportunities

Children’s artworks, stories, poems, ideas, and creations may be featured in Goppoguro books, magazines, exhibitions, or digital publications.

Creative Events

Story circles, workshops, festivals, play sessions, and community gatherings.
Recognition & Appreciation
Celebrating imagination, effort, kindness, and originality — not only “winning.”

Why Golpoka Club?

In a world filled with screens and passive entertainment, children need spaces where they can imagine, make, explore, and express.

Golpoka Club encourages children to:
Think creatively
Play positively
Build confidence
Develop imagination
Improve expression and communication
Learn through hands-on exploration
Create meaningful things
Feel proud of their ideas
We believe every child deserves opportunities to explore their own creative world.
